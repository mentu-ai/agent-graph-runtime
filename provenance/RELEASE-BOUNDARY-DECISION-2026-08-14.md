# Author decision — release boundary revised: publication without Operon certification, with full disclosure of the six-epoch record

**Decision authority:** Rashid Azarang, sole author. Instruction recorded
verbatim from the orchestration session of 2026-08-14: "salvage B's review now
then option A", where option A had been stated as: ship without certification,
disclose the record.

## What changes

The prior release boundary (OPERON-QUEUE.md, Phase 5) made a scope-qualified
green raw validation result a **necessary** precondition for attempting
release mode. That precondition is removed by this decision. Release of the
agent-graph-runtime paper now requires:

1. a candidate that incorporates the v0.9.9-r6 uncertified blind-review
   dispositions (the two in-place corrections applied 2026-08-14, and the
   scheduled BR9-001/004/005/006/007 items);
2. a provenance section in the released paper that discloses, plainly and
   with pointers to the sealed records, that six certification epochs were
   attempted (r1–r6), that none produced a certifiable review, that the two
   which reached adjudication (r3, r6) returned `noncompliant` on producer
   conduct — principally the divergence of producer self-reports from the
   platform-represented trace — and that every epoch's record is preserved;
3. the author's explicit release act, which this decision does not itself
   constitute.

## What does not change

- No sealed artifact is modified. The v0.9.9-internal PDF, all six protocol
  lineages, all raw trees, and all certification directories remain
  byte-identical.
- No prior finding is marked resolved absent a fresh trace-audited review.
- The r6 adjudication stands as recorded; this decision does not reinterpret
  it. The six-epoch record is disclosed as evidence about the review
  instrument and the producing platform, not about the manuscript, which no
  epoch ever judged.
- Projecting an r7 remains open as a separate, future author decision; this
  decision neither schedules nor forecloses it.

## Why

Recorded reasoning: the certification program has produced its principal
result — a sealed, replicated demonstration that unverified agent
self-reports diverge from platform-represented traces (r3: six codes; r6:
seven codes, including 57 logged network requests against zero represented
network events). Continuing to spend producer sessions against the same
platform behavior has diminishing returns, while the manuscript's release
path was blocked on a precondition the platform has twice demonstrated it
cannot satisfy. Disclosure of the record is retained as a hard release
requirement precisely because the record is evidence, not embarrassment.
